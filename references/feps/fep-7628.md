# FEP-7628: Move actor

FEP-7628 describes migrating followers from one actor to another with the `Move` activity. It documents what Mastodon has done since 2019 and generalises how the old and new actors are linked. See [identity](../../fundamentals/identity.md) for how accounts are named.

## What it defines

- Actors are **linked** when they represent one subject: an `alsoKnownAs` property (the most common method), FEP-c390 identity proofs, or verified rel-me links.
- Exactly one linked actor is the **primary**; migration means changing the primary.
- A `Move` activity has `id`, `type: Move`, `actor`, `object` (the old primary) and `target` (the new primary), and is valid only if `object` and `target` are linked.
- On a valid `Move`, followers of `object` MAY unfollow with `Undo(Follow)` and MUST either send `Follow` to `target` or tell their user the contact moved.
- Two modes: push (sent by the old actor, addressed to its followers) and pull (sent by the new actor, addressed to its followers; the old server need not be online). `[spec]`

## Who implements it

> Unknown: per-version behaviour. Mastodon is named by the FEP as the origin of the behaviour; how each studied server verifies a `Move`, handles the link check and treats followers is not yet read from pinned source.

## Technical details

The validity rule (linked actors) is the security-relevant part: without it anyone could announce a move for an account they do not control.
